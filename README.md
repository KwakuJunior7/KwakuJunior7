## Junior Owusu Ansah

I work where finance meets machine learning: credit and financial risk, fraud detection, and how far predictive models can be trusted on data from a later period than the one they were trained on.

M.S. Computer Science (expected December 2026) and MBA in Finance, New Mexico Highlands University.

### Research

- **Detecting illicit Bitcoin addresses with machine learning** (M.S. thesis, 2026). Five models on the Elliptic++ address data. XGBoost reached a PR-AUC of 0.971 on a random split, but F1 fell from 0.912 to 0.318 on later time periods, and part of the random-split score came from time-related features.
- **Predicting financial stability and Title IV compliance risk in New Mexico public colleges with graph neural networks.** Sole author; under review at the *Journal of Educational Data Mining*.
- **Assessing the business environment of Côte d'Ivoire: country profile and cocoa-processing industry analysis.** First author; accepted, *International Journal of Research in Management*.
